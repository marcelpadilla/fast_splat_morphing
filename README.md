Supplementary material for the preprint **Fast Splat Morphing**. FSM morphs one 3D Gaussian splatting capture into another through a near-optimal bijective assignment between their splats, built in the browser in about a second.

Pick two objects and the assignment between them is computed live, in your browser:

```bash
python demo/serve.py        # http://127.0.0.1:8000/
```

`demo/pairings.js` is the method, the co-bisection that builds the assignment. `demo/cloud.js` prepares the two clouds (outlier cull, normalization, padding the smaller side with transparent clones) and `demo/trajectories.js` bends each pair's path; `demo/renderer.js` is the viewer, adapted from [antimatter15/splat](https://github.com/antimatter15/splat) (MIT). These four files are the ones the paper's timings were measured with. The time the demo shows covers the same step the paper times: packing both clouds and building the assignment, single-threaded.

The splats keep their own terms. The house plant is the author's capture (CC BY 4.0). The bonsai and the duck were generated by the author with TripoSplat from CC0 photographs on Wikimedia Commons, by Wilfredor and by Oleg Yunakov, and are provided as-is, with no warranty of rights. All four are also in the author's [splat collection](https://github.com/marcelpadilla/splats) or derived from it.
